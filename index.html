Meet the Owner

A journey built one client at a time

Jyoti Shetty founded Star Parlour with a simple belief — that great beauty work is equal parts skill and listening. Trained across bridal makeup, advanced hair styling and skin care, She has spent over a decade perfecting a craft that's as much about how a client feels as how they look.

Every appointment at Star Parlour is shaped around that philosophy — unhurried consultations, techniques kept current with ongoing training, and an eye for detail that regulars have come to rely on.

About Star Parlour

Our story

Star Parlour opened its doors with one goal — to bring salon-quality bridal, hair and beauty care to our community, without the wait or the guesswork. The parlour was started in 2012.

Our mission

To make every client feel confident and cared for, using techniques and products we'd recommend to our own family.

Our facilities

A calm, hygienic studio space with dedicated stations for bridal makeup, hair styling and skin treatments.
